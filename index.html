10 years old • Andy 🐶
Andy's story

She has given us 10 beautiful years. Now she needs us.

Andy was brought to the veterinarian after developing blood in her urine. Her tests and X-ray found multiple stones in her urinary bladder.

🩸

What happened?

Andy presented with hematuria (blood in the urine). Her abdominal radiograph showed a markedly distended urinary bladder containing multiple radiopaque stones.

The veterinary diagnosis is cystolithiasis — urinary bladder stones.

Why we are fundraising

Andy needs surgery.

The radiology report recommends cystotomy for surgical removal of the bladder stones, particularly because of the large number of stones and bladder distension.

Because Andy is a senior dog and will need general anesthesia, her veterinarian also requested a cardiac assessment before surgery.
